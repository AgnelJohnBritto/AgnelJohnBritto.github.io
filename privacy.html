Privacy Policy

Your prayers and your privacy, both protected.

Last updated: July 26, 2026

Overview

Rosary – ஜெபமாலை ("the App") is a Tamil & English Catholic prayer app published on Google Play. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with the App. We built Rosary to be simple, offline, and respectful of your privacy from the start.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The App:

  • Works fully offline — no internet connection is required to use it.
  • Does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal details.
  • Does not contain third-party advertising.
  • Does not use analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting services.
  • Does not access your contacts, location, camera, or microphone.

All prayers, text, and audio content are bundled with the App itself and are accessed entirely on your device.

Donations

The App and this website may display a UPI QR code and UPI ID so users can voluntarily support us. If you choose to donate, that transaction is handled entirely by your own UPI app (such as Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm) and your bank. We do not receive, process, or store any payment, card, or bank account information — we only see that a donation was made, in the same way any UPI recipient would.

Third-Party Services

The App is distributed through Google Play, which has its own privacy practices governing installation, updates, and (if applicable) any permissions Google Play itself requires. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for details on how Google Play handles data.

Links from this website (such as our Instagram page or Google Play listing) lead to third-party services with their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review.

Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, since no personal data is collected by the App at all.

Data Security

Because the App does not collect or transmit personal data, there is no user data held by us that could be exposed in a data breach.

Changes to This Policy

If our data practices ever change (for example, if we introduce a new feature that requires data collection), we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above accordingly.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us at athisayapanimathashrine@gmail.com.
